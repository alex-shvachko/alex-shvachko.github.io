# Rover-to-forest storyboard

The files in this folder are the approved story order. The numeric prefix is the playback order.

1. `01_paper-rover-opening.png` — paper frame on the deep-green page.
2. `02_white-frame-front-rover.png` — full-white transition, front three-quarter rover.
3. `03_camera-orbit-right-inbetween.png` — camera moves partway around the rover's right side.
4. `04_rear-view-greenery-ground.png` — rear view with greenery covering the ground.
5. `05_more-greenery-trees-begin.png` — greenery thickens and young trees appear.
6. `06_camera-rises-trees-spread.png` — camera rises and rotates as the white space recedes.
7. `07_high-zoomout-rover-forest.png` — higher, farther view with the rover still consistent.
8. `08_aerial-rover-starts-downtrail.png` — aerial motion begins near the top of the trail.
9. `09_aerial-rover-midtrail.png` — rover reaches the middle of the trail.
10. `10_aerial-rover-lowertrail.png` — rover reaches the lower trail.
11. `11_locked-final-aerial-forest.png` — locked final aerial forest composition.

Only the latest accepted version of each stage is included. Superseded, duplicate, failed, and rejected generations remain outside this folder for traceability.
